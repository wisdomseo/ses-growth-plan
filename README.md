# SES Auto Growth Plan

Interactive plan for SES MGT: where the next auto-business clients will come from, what each channel can deliver, what it costs, and the decisions to make now.

**Open the page:** https://wisdomseo.github.io/ses-growth-plan/

- `index.html` + `assets/`: the page (no build step, no tracking). Every number lives in `assets/data.js` with its source.
- `research/`: the desk research behind the numbers (Google audience targeting, Meta and LinkedIn, outbound, partners and events), read Oct 25, 2026. Internal figures have been removed.
- Inputs, ticks, decisions and notes typed on the page are saved only in the viewer's own browser.

Benchmarks are mostly vendor-published and not auto-specific; SES has not tested most of these channels yet. The 90-day plan exists to replace them with SES's own numbers.
